Pubgolf Utrecht

Two teams of two play 10 pubs and 8 Albert Heijns through the old town. One sip is one stroke, and the Albert Heijn holes keep it cheap, and the lowest card at Café 't Pandje wins.

  • 18 holes
  • Par 72
  • 2 × 2 players
  • Tee off 14:30
  • Finish ~21:35

The route

Start at the top of the Oudegracht and work past the Neude to the station, where you buy dinner at Albert Heijn. After dinner, walk down the Oudegracht to the Twijnstraat, cross east to the Nachtegaalstraat and Biltstraat, and finish on the Nobelstraat.

North is up. Lines run straight between stops, and pins around the Neude and the Nobelstraat are nudged apart so you can read them. Use the buttons for walking directions.

    Power-ups

    Each team draws two before hole 1. A team can draw one more for an eagle or better, and the team behind at The Turn draws one too. Each card works once, and a team can play one card per hole. Change the strokes on the card yourself when a power-up changes them.

    Rules

    How a hole works

    • Each team shares one drink per hole and teammates alternate sips. On the jenever and shot holes, each player gets their own.
    • A stroke is one sip by either teammate. The glass leaving your lips ends the sip.
    • Both teams start together. Finish the drink, write the score, take your glass back to the bar and move on.
    • The other team counts your sips. Disputes go to the bartender if they're up for it.
    • Lowest total after 18 wins. If it's level, the team with more birdies wins. If that's level too, you share the title.

    Albert Heijn holes

    • Each team buys the drink on the card, pays, and plays the hole just outside the shop. Supermarket prices are what keep the day cheap.
    • Bring ID. Albert Heijn asks anyone who looks under 25.
    • Supermarkets in the city centre stop selling alcohol at 22:00. The last AH hole is at 20:15, so you're well inside that.
    • Drinking alcohol on the street is banned in a large part of the city centre, and you can be fined for it. Check the city's map of alcohol-free zones before the day and play these holes where it's allowed.
    • Cans and bottles go in a bin or back to the shop for the deposit. Littering is +3.

    Free passes

    • Any player can swap any drink for water or 0.0 at any time, with no penalty.
    • If a pub is full or closed, each team takes par and moves on, or plays a backup from the plan.

    Penalties

    Spilling+1
    Sipping out of turn+1
    Holding the glass in your dominant hand+1
    Leaving your glass on the table+1
    Emptying a beer, wine or cocktail in one go+2
    Littering a can, bottle or wrapper+3
    Being rude to staff+5 and you buy them a drink

    The plan

    On the day, based on a Saturday

    1. 14:15Meet at Café Weerdzicht, Oudegracht 1. Fill in the team names and draw power-ups.
    2. 14:30Tee off at hole 1. Pub holes get about 18 minutes and AH holes about 10, plus the walk. The times on the card include both.
    3. 15:30Reach the Neude for holes 4 and 5.
    4. 16:50Play two Albert Heijns by the station: Hoog Catharijne, then Stationsplein.
    5. 17:05Hole 9 at AH Stationsplein: play the Grolsch and buy a broodje each for dinner. Break until 18:05. The team behind draws a power-up.
    6. 18:05Start the back nine at The Postillon on the Lijnmarkt.
    7. 18:55The longest walk comes next: from the Twijnstraat to the Nachtegaalstraat, about 18 minutes.
    8. 21:15Play hole 18 at Café 't Pandje, which opens at 21:00, then add up the card. The losers buy the winners' last round.

    Before you go

    • Check opening hours that week. Bar Vivid opens at 17:30 and Café 't Pandje at 21:00, and three of the Albert Heijns close at 21:00, so this order is built for a Saturday. AH Voorstraat is closed on Sundays.
    • There's no restaurant on the route. Dinner is a broodje you buy at AH Stationsplein on hole 9, so eat a proper lunch first.
    • Bring a bank card. Albert Heijn takes debit cards and Mastercard, but not every foreign card.
    • Budget roughly €40–50 per person. The Albert Heijn holes cost a euro or two per person. That's an estimate, not a menu price.
    • Plan your way home and don't drive. Utrecht Centraal is about a 15-minute walk from the Nobelstraat.

    Pacing

    Every hole has alcohol, so pace it. Sharing a drink keeps each player to about half of what's on the card. Drink water between holes, eat the broodje, and drop out or swap to 0.0 whenever you want. Eat a proper lunch, have water between holes, and drop out or swap to 0.0 whenever you want.

    Backups

    • For the front nine: Bierlokaal de Luifel (Neude 35-36) or Café de Zaak (Korte Minrebroederstraat 9).
    • For the back nine: Café Jan de Winter (Oudegracht 170) or Theatercafé The Bastard (Jansveld 17).
    • If an Albert Heijn is closed: AH to go in the station hall stays open until 01:00.
