The plan

On the day, based on a Saturday

  1. 14:15Meet at Café Weerdzicht, Oudegracht 1. Fill in the team names and draw power-ups.
  2. 14:30Tee off at hole 1. Pub holes get about 18 minutes and AH holes about 10, plus the walk. The times on the card include both.
  3. 15:30Reach the Neude for holes 4 and 5.
  4. 16:50Play two Albert Heijns by the station: Hoog Catharijne, then Stationsplein.
  5. 17:05Hole 9 at AH Stationsplein: play the Grolsch and buy a broodje each for dinner. Break until 18:05. The team behind draws a power-up.
  6. 18:05Start the back nine at The Postillon on the Lijnmarkt.
  7. 18:55The longest walk comes next: from the Twijnstraat to the Nachtegaalstraat, about 18 minutes.
  8. 21:15Play hole 18 at Café 't Pandje, which opens at 21:00, then add up the card. The losers buy the winners' last round.

Before you go

  • Check opening hours that week. Bar Vivid opens at 17:30 and Café 't Pandje at 21:00, and three of the Albert Heijns close at 21:00, so this order is built for a Saturday. AH Voorstraat is closed on Sundays.
  • There's no restaurant on the route. Dinner is a broodje you buy at AH Stationsplein on hole 9, so eat a proper lunch first.
  • Bring a bank card. Albert Heijn takes debit cards and Mastercard, but not every foreign card.
  • Budget roughly €40–50 per person. The Albert Heijn holes cost a euro or two per person. That's an estimate, not a menu price.
  • Plan your way home and don't drive. Utrecht Centraal is about a 15-minute walk from the Nobelstraat.

Pacing

Every hole has alcohol, so pace it. Sharing a drink keeps each player to about half of what's on the card. Drink water between holes, eat the broodje, and drop out or swap to 0.0 whenever you want.

Backups

  • For the front nine: Bierlokaal de Luifel (Neude 35-36) or Café de Zaak (Korte Minrebroederstraat 9).
  • For the back nine: Café Jan de Winter (Oudegracht 170) or Theatercafé The Bastard (Jansveld 17).
  • If an Albert Heijn is closed: AH to go in the station hall stays open until 01:00.
