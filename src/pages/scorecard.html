Scorecard

Count your team's sips on each hole. Every hole is par 1, so the first tap on + or − gives 1. When a hole is done, tap Lock at the end of its row (twice, to confirm) to lock in both teams' scores. A locked hole can't be changed.

Scores are saved on this device. Keep the card on one phone.
