The route

Start at the top of the Oudegracht and work past the Neude to the station, where you buy dinner at Albert Heijn. After dinner, walk down the Oudegracht to the Twijnstraat, cross east to the Nachtegaalstraat and Biltstraat, and finish on the Nobelstraat.
