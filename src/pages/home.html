Pubgolf Utrecht

Two teams of two play 10 pubs and 8 Albert Heijns through the old town. One sip is one stroke, and the Albert Heijn holes keep it cheap, and the lowest card at Café 't Pandje wins.

  • 18 holes
  • Par 18
  • 2 × 2 players
  • Tee off 14:30
  • Finish ~21:35
