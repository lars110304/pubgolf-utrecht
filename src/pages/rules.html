Rules

A hole

  • One drink per team, teammates alternate sips. Jenever and shots: one each.
  • One sip is one stroke. Every hole is par 1.
  • The other team counts your sips.
  • Lowest total wins. Tie: most birdies.

Albert Heijn

  • Buy the drink, play it outside. Bring ID.
  • No drinking in the alcohol-free zones.

Free passes

  • Swap for water or 0.0 any time, no penalty.
  • Pub full or closed: take par or use a backup.
