Minigames

Between the bars. Low score wins, so minus is good.

  1. Rock paper scissors

    The winner sets an extra rule for the next bar, like "left hand only" or "no names".

    Breaking the rule+1

  2. No reaction shot

    The bartender pours a clear shot and a water. Team A drinks, team B guesses who had the real shot.

    Team B guesses right: team A+2

    Team B guesses wrong: team B+2

  3. Sprite challenge

    Down half a litre of Sprite in one go.

    You make it−3

    You don't, per sip+1

  4. Statues

    At a random moment the pubgolf leader shouts "Freeze".

    Last one to stand still+1

  5. Categories

    Pick a theme, like beer brands, and take turns naming one.

    Hesitate or repeat: a sip and+1
